About KookaKlunka Studios, LLC

The company behind Klunk - Log Viewer, and how it operates.

What KookaKlunka is

KookaKlunka Studios, LLC is an independent software company. Its current product is Klunk - Log Viewer, a desktop application for reading logs from local files, databases, Kubernetes pods, SFTP servers, and HTTP endpoints in one place. Why the name KookaKlunka? It's just a made-up word that my nephew wanted to name his sister.

Who's behind it

KookaKlunka was founded by Michael Dow and remains a one-person operation. It is an independent, self-funded company — no investors, no outside stakeholders, just one developer and the users who send in bug reports and ideas.
